Questions patients ask

How do I book an appointment?

Message us on WhatsApp at 0306 1521205 or call the same number. Tell us what you need and we will suggest a time.

Is dental treatment painful?

We use local anesthesia where needed and focus on patient comfort. Tell us if you feel anxious and we will go at your pace.

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people benefit from a check-up and cleaning every six months, or sooner if you have pain, bleeding gums or a damaged tooth.

Do you replace missing teeth?

Yes. Options include dental implants, zirconia bridges and dentures. We will explain which suits your situation.

Can I get a price before treatment?

Yes. After your exam we explain the treatment plan and its cost before we start.
